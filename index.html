Mumbai DP 2034 Explorer

An interactive map of the Development Plan 2034 of the Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai, built from the city's public GIS service.

  • Click any spot for a location report: the property parcel (CTS/CS number, village, area, DP remarks) together with its land-use zone, reservations, designations, road proposals, CRZ category, heritage listing, ward and village.
  • Search a CTS/CS number, village or ward to fly to a plot.
  • Toggle layers and switch basemaps from the left panel. The map position is kept in the address bar, so a URL reopens the same view.
